North Coast Community Watershed Project

Drinking water on the north coast

How people get water in Clatsop and Tillamook, creek by creek. Public records from September 4, 2026. This is not who drinks from what, and it is not a spray map yet.

Read the caveats

Each shape is a creek-sized watershed that touches one or both counties. The three lists are houses outside city limits, Oregon well logs, and legal rights to take water from a stream. A house outside city limits can still sit on a city or district pipe. A well with no stream right is often groundwater, or it may predate the law. Year built, well date, pipes, and forestry spray are not on this map yet.
